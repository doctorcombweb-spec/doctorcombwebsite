The portal provides a clean, secure content dashboard designed for non-technical clinic staff, doctors, and copywriters. Your team writes in an intuitive interface similar to Google Docs or Notion, while high-performance code compiles automatically.

1

Article Title & Automated SEO Slug Permalinks

Typing your headline (e.g., "Non-Surgical Hair Patch in Delhi: 2026 Price Guide") automatically cleans and outputs an optimized URL slug: /hair-patch-delhi-guide.html. No manual coding or URL formatting required.

2

Featured Cover Visuals with Smart Hotspot Cropping

Upload real before/after client images or clinic facility photos. Sanity’s visual hotspot tool guarantees your photos frame properly across vertical smartphone screens, tablets, and wide desktop displays without awkward clipping.

3

Rich Text Formatting & "Key Takeaway" Callout Box

Supports structured H2/H3 subheadings, numbered lists, bullet points, and quotes. Includes a special summary callout field displayed prominently at the top of the post to maximize on-page dwell time.

4

Author Verification & Medical E-E-A-T Standards

Assign each post to senior trichologists or stylists (e.g., Dr. Meera Sharma, Chief Hair Specialist). This automatically satisfies Google’s strict E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) criteria for cosmetic health content.

5

One-Click Publish with Instant Background Compilation

Clicking "Publish" immediately triggers our static compiler. There are zero manual exports, zero FTP uploads, and zero database administration headaches.

2 Why Every Single Page Is 100% Pure Static HTML
The Speed & Indexing Breakthrough: Unlike WordPress (which executes heavy PHP code and queries a database on every visitor hit) or client-side JavaScript apps (which force Googlebot to wait for JavaScript to execute), our build script compiles every blog article directly into a pure, pre-rendered .html file.

3 Comprehensive SEO Metric Compliance Matrix

Every compiled article automatically integrates all technical ranking criteria enforced by Google’s 2026 search algorithm:

🏷️
Dynamic Meta Titles & Descriptions
Auto-generated with intent-driven keywords and location branding: [Title] | AURA Hair Clinic Delhi. Optimizes search engine Click-Through Rate (CTR).
🔗
Automated Canonical Tags
Embeds <link rel="canonical"> targeting the authoritative clean URL, shielding rankings against duplicate content penalties from campaign tracking parameters.
🧬
JSON-LD Structured Data Schema
Injects Google-compliant BlogPosting and LocalBusiness schema specifying author credentials, publish date, and Delhi clinic coordinates for Google Rich Snippets.
📱
OpenGraph & Twitter Rich Cards
Fully styled social previews for WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X. When an article is shared, it renders with full-bleed cover imagery, headline, and excerpt.
⚡
Native Image Lazy Loading
All in-content media uses browser-native loading="lazy" with descriptive alt-attributes to protect mobile bandwidth and eliminate Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) delays.
🌐
Topic Clustering & Internal Linking
Each page automatically renders a "Popular Hair Guides" sidebar and 3 contextual "Related Articles" cards, passing link authority across your entire catalog.
