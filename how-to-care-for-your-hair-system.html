How to Care for Your Hair System

Dr
Dr. Meera Sharma
Chief Hair Specialist • Doctor Comb Lajpat Nagar

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY AT A GLANCE

With cool or lukewarm water, sulfate-free moisturizing cleansers, and downward gliding motions, your hair system will retain its softness, shine, and knot strength for 10 to 14 months.

The Golden Rules of Hair System Longevity

Unlike natural hair attached to sebaceous glands, hair replacement systems do not receive natural scalp sebum. This means you must nourish the 100% human hair cuticles externally with the right products and techniques.

1. The Correct Washing Technique

  • Always Use Lukewarm or Cool Water: Hot water can soften the acrylic bonding polymer and loosen delicate lace hand-tied knots.
  • Never Scrub in Circles: Apply sulfate-free shampoo to wet palms and gently smooth downward from crown to ends. Circular rubbing causes tangling.
  • Deep Condition the Mid-Lengths: Keep heavy conditioner 0.5 inches away from the base mesh to prevent loosening the knot adhesives.

2. Daily Detangling & Brushing

Always use a wide-tooth comb or loop brush specifically engineered for hair systems. Start combing gently from the tips and work your way up toward the roots while supporting the hair base with your free hand.

3. Night Care & Silk Pillowcases

Cotton pillowcases create friction that can cause static and split ends. Switching to a mulberry silk or satin pillowcase preserves cuticle moisture and keeps the hairline intact through the night.
