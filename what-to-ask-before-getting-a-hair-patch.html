What to Ask Before Getting a Hair Patch

Dr
Dr. Meera Sharma
Chief Hair Specialist • Doctor Comb Lajpat Nagar

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY AT A GLANCE

Before investing in a hair system, ask 5 essential questions: Is the hair 100% Remy Human Hair? What is the base breathability? Are consultations private? What are the recurring service charges? And is there a trial guarantee?

Be an Informed Buyer in Delhi NCR

With hundreds of roadside salons advertising hair patches at unrealistic prices, asking the right technical questions protects you from synthetic fiber traps and poor hairline knotting.

The 5 Vital Questions to Ask Any Clinic

  • 1. Is the hair 100% Remy Virgin Human Hair? Cheap salons often blend 30-50% synthetic fiber that turns brittle and shiny after two washes. Always demand 100% human cuticle-aligned Remy hair.
  • 2. Which Base Material Suits Indian Climates? In Delhi's 45°C summers, French Lace or Silk Base provides vital scalp ventilation, whereas standard thick poly bases can trap perspiration.
  • 3. Does the Front Hairline Feature Bleached Knots? Bleached single-knot front hairlines mimic hairs growing directly out of your scalp pores under bright sunlight and HD cameras.
  • 4. Do You Provide 100% Private Cabins? Privacy is paramount. Ensure your measurements, fitting, and haircut occur in a dedicated, soundproof private room.
  • 5. What Are the Exact Monthly Service Costs? Clarify maintenance fees upfront. At Doctor Comb, routine 4-week servicing is transparent with zero hidden costs.
