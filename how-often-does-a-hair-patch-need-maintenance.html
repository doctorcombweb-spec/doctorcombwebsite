How Often Does a Hair Patch Need Maintenance?

Dr
Dr. Meera Sharma
Chief Hair Specialist • Doctor Comb Lajpat Nagar

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY AT A GLANCE

Hair systems require servicing every 3 to 4 weeks. In a 45-minute clinic session, the base is sanitized and refreshed, your biological hair underneath is trimmed, and new medical bonding is applied.

Why Regular Servicing Matters

Even though hair systems do not grow, your biological hair underneath continues its natural growth cycle of roughly half an inch per month. Over 3 to 4 weeks, this natural growth, combined with sweat and daily washes, gently loosens perimeter adhesives.

What Happens During a 45-Minute Maintenance Session?

  • Gentle System Detachment: Safe release sprays dissolve the perimeter bond painlessly without pulling a single strand of hair.
  • Scalp Cleansing & Exfoliation: Your scalp is thoroughly shampooed, exfoliated, and massaged with botanical tonics to prevent dandruff and itchiness.
  • Natural Hair Grooming: Your side and back biological hair is neatly trimmed and blended to match your desired fade or sideburn length.
  • Base Deep Sanitization: The base mesh is deep-cleansed, adhesive residue is removed, and hair strands receive a keratin conditioning treatment.
  • Fresh Re-Attachment & Styling: New medical adhesive or tapes are applied, precision-aligned to your natural front hairline, and styled to perfection.
