Hair Bonding vs Hair Taping

AK
Amit Kapoor
Master Stylist & Patch Specialist • Delhi NCR

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY AT A GLANCE

Hair bonding uses medical liquid adhesives for 4-6 weeks of uninterrupted security, whereas hair taping uses gentle hypoallergenic double-sided strips lasting 2-4 weeks, ideal for simple at-home removal and sensitive scalps.

The Core Difference: Liquid Bond vs Adhesive Tape

When selecting a non-surgical hair replacement system, attachment method is everything. Both bonding and taping provide an undetectable, secure hold, but they cater to very different daily routines and scalp types.

1. Hair Bonding (Medical Liquid Acrylics & Silicones)

In hair bonding, a medical-grade liquid adhesive (such as Ghost Bond or Ultra Hold) is applied evenly along the shaved perimeter or scalp area. Once cured transparent, the lace or poly base is locked down firmly.

  • Lifespan of Hold: 4 to 6 weeks before requiring service.
  • Best For: High-energy active individuals, gym-goers, swimmers, and heavy sweaters in Delhi's summer heat.
  • Feel: Feels like a permanent part of your scalp with zero perimeter movement.

2. Hair Taping (Medical Double-Sided Strips)

Taping uses pre-cut contoured hypoallergenic tape rolls (such as Walker No-Shine or Supertape) placed around the perimeter base borders.

  • Lifespan of Hold: 2 to 4 weeks.
  • Best For: First-time wearers, clients with sensitive scalps, and individuals who want the option of easy home maintenance.
  • Ease of Removal: Releases in under 2 minutes with medical citrus solvent without sticky residue.

Which One Should You Choose?

If you prefer visiting our salon once a month for a full luxury wash and re-fitting, Hair Bonding is your gold standard. If you want maximum breathability and lower maintenance overhead, Hair Taping is unbeatable.
